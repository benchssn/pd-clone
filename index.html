WELCOME TO PHASE

A small laboratory.
A lot to discover.

Inspired by Pure Data’s signal flow, Phase makes sound synthesis a little more approachable.

  1. Shape your sound. Pick a waveform and tune each oscillator. A is green; B is violet.
  2. Find a balance. Mix the two sources, then set the master volume. Drag the dial or use its slider.
  3. Listen and look. Start audio and watch the waveform. Switch to Spectrum to see its frequencies.
